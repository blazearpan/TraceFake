# TraceFake Viva Preparation

The detailed viva bank should be generated after the implementation and actual results exist, so answers about metrics, model selection, dataset size after deduplication, and feature importance are based on the student's real run rather than invented values.

Core questions to prepare:
- What is phishing?
- Why can URL features identify suspicious links?
- Why use a hybrid detector?
- Why is accuracy alone insufficient?
- What are precision, recall, F1, and ROC-AUC?
- What is data leakage?
- Why split into train, validation, and test sets?
- Why does TraceFake not open URLs?
- Why is the risk score not called probability?
- What are false positives and false negatives?
- Why is explainability important?
- Why use SQLite?
- What are the project's limitations?
